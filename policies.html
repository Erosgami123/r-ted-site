What this website does

Red-Ted presents ecommerce consulting services. There are no customer accounts, online orders, checkout, payment collection or contact-form submissions on this website.

The site does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, or store information in your browser.

Email contact

Contact links open your own email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send an email. Your email service and the recipient's email service process the message independently of this website.

Please send only information relevant to your enquiry, and do not include card details, passwords or sensitive personal documents.

For questions about this website or an email enquiry, contact support@r-ted.site.

Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical access information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of providing hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.
